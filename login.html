Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Welcome to CampusEase, the official smart administrative services and student portal for Riara University. By creating an account or accessing our services, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms and Conditions.

1. Account Responsibilities

To access CampusEase, you must be a registered student, faculty member, or administrative staff member with valid university credentials.

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login password and account credentials.
  • You agree not to share your account access with unauthorized third parties.
  • Any administrative requests, queue reservations, or assignment submissions made through your account are deemed to be submitted by you.

2. Virtual Queue & Appointment Policies

CampusEase provides remote queuing and administrative appointment scheduling for campus offices (Registrar, Financial Aid, IT Helpdesk, Housing, etc.).

  • Students must arrive at the designated department within 5 minutes of receiving their "Approaching Counter" notification.
  • Intentional abuse of the virtual queue system (e.g., generating false tickets without intent to attend) may result in temporary queue suspension.
  • Appointments may be canceled or rescheduled up to 1 hour prior to the scheduled slot without penalty.

3. Academic Integrity & Submissions

Assignments and coursework uploaded via the Assignments Portal must strictly comply with university academic honesty guidelines.

  • Files submitted must be free of malicious software, viruses, or corrupted data.
  • Submissions are logged with automated timestamping reflecting the exact time of upload.

4. Privacy & Data Handling

Your privacy is important to us. CampusEase collects personal academic records, attendance records, and service interactions strictly for educational and administrative support.

5. Service Availability & Modifications

Riara University reserves the right to modify, suspend, or update CampusEase service modules during scheduled maintenance windows.
