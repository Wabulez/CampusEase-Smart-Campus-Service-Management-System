Services Directory FAQ

How do virtual queues work?

Join the queue online from your phone or laptop. You will receive an SMS when you are 3rd in line so you don't have to wait physically in line.

What if I need to cancel an appointment?

Navigate to the My Appointments tab to reschedule or cancel up to 1 hour before your slot.

How fast are IT tickets resolved?

Standard password and Wi-Fi tickets under Eng. Victor Stone are resolved within 2 business hours.
